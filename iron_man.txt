Tony Stark
The invincible Iron Man is an armored golden avenger who uses advanced technology to adventure the world. Genius billionaire inventor 
Tony Stark
 built the first 
Iron Man Armor
 to escape captivity in 
Sin-Cong
 and keep his heart being to stay alive. Ever since, as owner of 
Stark Industries
, Stark has acted as a self-regarded but prestigious super hero, originally disguising Iron Man as his bodyguard. Stark's best friend, Colonel Jim Rhodes, who suits up as 
War Machine
, carried on the duty of being Iron Man. In a journey for redemption, the super villain 
Doctor Doom
 infamously became Iron Man as well. The identity was usurped by Stark's adopted brother 
Arno Stark
, whose tenure ignited a 
rebel revolution
 led by the original Iron Man.
